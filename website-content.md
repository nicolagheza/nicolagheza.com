## Services

Leveraging expertise in AI, data science, and robust backend engineering, I offer specialized services to help businesses leverage technology for improved efficiency, insights, and automation. Below are the key service areas I provide:

### Backend Engineering

Building the foundational server-side logic, databases, and APIs that power your applications and data services.

- **Custom API Development:** Design and implement robust and scalable APIs to connect your front-end applications with backend services and data.
- **Database Design & Management:** Create, optimize, and manage databases to store and retrieve data efficiently for your applications and AI models.
- **Server-Side Logic:** Develop the core business logic and processes that run on the server, ensuring performance and reliability.
- **System Integration:** Connect disparate systems and services to enable seamless data flow and functionality.
- **Scalable Architecture:** Design and build backend systems that can handle increasing amounts of data and user traffic.

### Computer Vision

Solutions to help you leverage your visual data and automatically analyze video or image streams.

- **Media Asset Retrieval:** Quickly retrieve images whose contents match your textual query. No more digging through folders and relying on filenames—just type "balloons" to find all images containing balloons.
- **AI-Powered Video Analytics:**
  - **CCTV Footage Analysis:** Automatically process and extract relevant events from CCTV footage, detect suspicious movements, and extract valuable business insights like customer counts and peak hours.
  - **Footage Anonymization:** Comply with GDPR rules by automatically blurring sensitive information from camera footage, such as faces and number plates.
  - **Quality Control Solutions:** Build pipelines to automatically detect anomalies in industrial goods, produce, or other products.

### General Purpose AI

- **Knowledge Base Building:** Custom ChatGPT-like agents that know your company and business inside out, helping you quickly retrieve information and discover new insights.
  - Leverage all company documents (PDFs, spreadsheets, videos, etc.)
  - Integrate with your company's systems (CRM, databases, etc.)
  - Deploy in a secure cloud environment for data privacy
- **Accounting Tools:** Process paper invoices and receipts from different suppliers, extracting relevant information and automatically categorizing by expense category.
  - Process various document types and extract information
  - Automatically anonymize documents by removing sensitive information

### Miscellaneous Data Services

- **Data Visualization Dashboards:** Build dashboards to collect and visualize static or live data for a clearer overview of your operations.
  - Track machinery operating status
  - Monitor consumables levels
  - Count visitors using computer vision
- **Legacy Software Maintenance:** Maintain, fix, or upgrade your AI or data-based systems to improve performance, reduce costs, or implement new features.
- **Data Scraping:** Build tools to automatically collect data from webpages, clean it, and store it in databases or spreadsheets, with optional notifications via email or text message.
- **Routing Tools:** Optimize delivery routes to save time, fuel, and money for businesses that fulfill a large number of deliveries daily.
